# Longer paired training: seed 0, generation 19 to 100

Author: Libor "Burny" Burian. Registered 2026-11-07 **after the first pilot's published results and before any continuation model execution**. This is a new, explicitly follow-up experiment, requested by the user. The [first registration](preregistration.md), checkpoints, deadlines and results remain unchanged. The first GitHub commit containing this file, [configuration](../configs/continuation.json), and [fresh manifest](../data/continuation/manifest.json) fixes this protocol.

## Motivation and source basis

The [completed pilot](experiment-pilot.md) did not show a main-versus-control accuracy advantage and its correct-response novelty signal was sparse. Longer optimization might allow the trajectories to diverge, or might preserve the null result, damage task performance, or overfit the fixed probes. These are alternatives to test, not claims that more generations will help. The duration is the sole training intervention. Retain the source-aligned weighting in Amendment A: population-standardized task reward plus unnormalized correctness-gated novelty, with no second normalization. Sources: [ES at Scale, Algorithm 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24372v3), [ExTra, Equation 2 and Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.24994v1), and the pinned [source correspondence audit](../docs/source-conformance.md). This remains an ES/ExTra adaptation; neither source validates this exact combination or predicts its result.

Exact inputs, formula and derived values are generated from the parent artifacts in `configs/continuation.json`. This is a duration chosen from timing evidence, not a result-dependent stopping rule. Stop at the fixed target or deadline even if a difference appears earlier. Incomplete arms remain explicitly incomplete and do not supply the primary comparison.

## Hypotheses and analysis

H1: final main accuracy exceeds final reward-only accuracy on the fresh set for this seed. A difference at or below zero fails that directional prediction. A paired percentile 95% problem-bootstrap interval wholly above zero is conditional evidence of benefit; one wholly below zero is conditional evidence of harm; an interval spanning zero leaves the sign unresolved. Report zero/negative effects and failures. Larger novelty or greater trajectory divergence alone does not establish task benefit. No claim of equivalence follows from a nonsignificant interval.

Use 10000 paired problem resamples, seed 20261107, preserving each problem's cross-method outcomes. Report both final methods and the original model. A secondary change contrast is `(main100 - main19) - (control100 - control19)` on the fresh set, resampling the four outcomes together. Also report each arm's own change, discordant correctness counts, validity, reward, harder accuracy, probe accuracy, sampled pass@1/pass@8, correct expression structures, novelty-active candidates and generations, runtime, tokens and truncations. Show cumulative and added-training costs separately. Secondary intervals are descriptive and unadjusted; no post-hoc significance selection. Bootstrap intervals **exclude training-seed variability**. This continuation cannot establish seed robustness and is explicitly motivated by an already-observed pilot. Excluding earlier multisets changes the finite sampling population slightly; do not treat accuracy differences between the old and fresh sets as a pure training effect.
