# Interpretation of the longer comparison

Libor "Burny" Burian · 2026-11-12. These notes were written after final evaluation. All empirical values and intervals are generated in the [experiment report](experiment-continuation-seed0-gen100.md); no endpoint, stopping rule or checkpoint was selected from these outcomes.

Longer training improved both arms relative to their earlier checkpoints on the same fresh problems. The novelty arm did not outperform reward-only ES on the registered fresh primary endpoint, and the paired interval includes zero. This is neither evidence of a novelty benefit nor proof of equivalence. The observed old-test difference favors reward-only ES, but that set had already been observed; its interval is descriptive, secondary and unadjusted. It cannot replace the fresh primary comparison. Both harder-problem performance and sampled expression diversity remain in the generated tables, irrespective of direction.

The bonus did change recorded main-arm update coefficients, so the outcome cannot be dismissed as an entirely inactive novelty intervention. The coefficient diagnostic compares weights on observed populations; it does not replay a counterfactual trajectory. More frequent novelty activity alone does not establish useful generalization, and these data do not identify why the added objective failed to improve the fresh endpoint. One paired seed cannot establish robustness or general failure of novelty methods.

The OS restart interrupted control training after the main arm had finished. The original continuation allowance expired. The [recovery registration](preregistration-recovery.md), copied source evidence and repeated response logs document the recovery. Its historical exact-state audit cannot replace missing initial recovery snapshots for an independent check today. The recovered result must not be described as meeting the earlier execution deadline. Resource tables distinguish cumulative tokens, added training and observed phase wall intervals; these intervals exclude offline time and the unknown unlogged outage tail, and are not GPU compute time.

The [historical publication audit](publication-audit-continuation-seed0-gen100.json) records remote hash checks, collection membership and publication timing. Independent current verification and its limits are recorded in the [dated corrections](independent-corrections-2026-11-12.md). The [restoration instructions](../docs/continuation.md) use a short configurable cache directory and include the separately published model exports.

The next scientific question is whether the observed comparison is stable across independent paired seeds, followed by targeted controls if warranted. Continuing this same seed until a favorable score appears would not answer that question. Later studies remain unexecuted, and their scope and budget require a new registration after the outage rather than an implicit extension of this result.
